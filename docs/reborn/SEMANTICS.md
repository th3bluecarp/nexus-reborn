# Semantics ledger

Every meaning we assigned to an undocumented field, enum value or mechanic, with the evidence, how sure we are, what else it could be and what would settle it. The code follows these entries; when one turns out wrong, fix the entry first, then the code it names.

Confidence: **High** = consistent across all the data we checked and no counter examples. **Medium** = consistent with the examples we looked at, alternatives not ruled out. **Low** = a guess that makes something playable, expect to revisit. **Invented** = no data at all, a tunable number.

- **A spell that fails to update is finished.** A spell whose update throws (an interaction whose target left the map before it finished) is finished and dropped with one error line (UnitEntity.Update). Retail would never have reached this state; without it the entity's whole update stopped for good, which read as a permanent global cooldown.

- **An auto-complete quest nobody gives is offered by its receiver.** Quests such as A Failed Experiment and Making Amends were started by using an object that lists them only as received; the quest has no objectives and the AutoComplete flag. The script that offered it is lost, so the receiving object offers it too (GlobalQuestManager.InitialiseQuestRelations): use it once to accept, once to complete.
- **A named monster drops its quest item every time.** Doomsting drops the Doomsting Stinger at 100% instead of the 9.71% Jabbithole share, because one Doomsting spawns in Celestion (`data/quest_fixes.sql`).

- **A type 10 step that names a creature counts its use.** Objective type 10 normally names a target group of things to use; when its Data is a Creature2 id (Greenbough Guardian's Firestorm Flingatrons, smashed in retail by a summoned Witch Giant) each direct use of that creature counts one (WorldEntity.OnActivateSuccess).
- **Lost quest givers are named by hand.** A quest no creature lists as given, whose text names who sent the player (A Captain in Need: Naida Zavlonov), gets that creature as its giver in GlobalQuestManager.LostQuestGivers.

- **EnterZone steps complete inside their indicator too.** The map files report a few sub-zones only as their parents (Darkloam Hole, the Augmentation Chamber), so an EnterZone objective completes either on the zone change or while the player stands inside the objective's map indicator (QuestManager.UpdateAreaObjectives), like EnterArea.
- **A quest ability that is a client-side interaction succeeds by itself.** Retail clients answered such casts (Fetish Creation in Moodoo Voodoo) with an interaction result; cast from the quest tracker there is no interaction, so the server runs the success after the cast time (SpellClientSideInteraction.Cast).
- **Using a non-unit object succeeds without spell targets.** Objects spawned as taxis or other non-unit kinds (the Fire Suppressor) are left out of a spell's target list; an Activate interaction on one succeeds directly (SpellClientSideInteraction.SucceedClientInteraction).
- **Hidden steps wait for the visible required steps, and never hide a quest giver.** A hidden textless step completes once every visible required step is done (optional steps may stay open); a visibility prerequisite that names such a step ignores its premature completion, and a creature named by an open TalkTo step is visible regardless (Consort Yaenna in Trials of a Matria).

## Damage formula

- **Crit severity** is CriticalHitSeverityMultiplier (UnitProperty2 default 1.5) plus the rating share from GameFormula 1232, capped at 300 percent. Critical mitigation scales only the bonus above 100 percent. Crit deflect and critical mitigation do not apply to heals. High confidence on the 1.5 base, Medium on how mitigation applies. Before this, the base was 0, so a crit with no severity rating did 0 damage.
- **Rating to percent conversion** (High, read from WildStar64.exe 0x140453b60, the routine every rating getter calls, and its cosf at 0x1408fc950): each point is worth GameFormula Datafloat0 / level x Datafloat01 percent plus Dataint0, up to the knee at Datafloat02 percent; past the knee the extra rating t adds cos(t / (t + Datafloat03) x pi/2) x t points worth (a soft cap); the result is a percentage, so x 0.01, then the base property is added and Dataint01 percent caps it. The crit severity getter reads property 47 as the rating and 127 (CriticalHitSeverityMultiplier) as the base. Upstream code left out the x 0.01, which made every combat rating 100 times too strong (a level 6 character crit for 2.5x raw from about 30 severity rating; it is now about 1.53x). Armor uses the same routine with knee 0.
- **Strikethrough** is subtracted from the target's deflect chance, **armor pierce** multiplies the armor part of mitigation by 1 minus its share (Medium, WildStar tooltips describe both this way, exact client math not read).
- **Multi-hit** adds MultiHitAmount share of the hit to the same hit instead of a separate strike (Invented presentation, the client has a MultiHit combat log we do not send).
- **Reflect** sends the reflect share of the damage taken straight to the attacker's health, unmitigated and not reflected again (Medium).
- **Creature spell effects honour their caster prerequisite.** Creature auto attacks carry two damage effects gated by Prerequisite type 179, the caster's Creature2Difficulty id against ObjectId (one coefficient for the low ranks, a bigger one for the high ranks; 224 auto-attack spells, 457 damage effects). The server only checked caster prerequisites for players, so creatures applied both effects and every hit landed twice (a level-4 creature hit a level-13 character for 89 and 129 raw per swing). For creature casters the difficulty checks are now evaluated and other prerequisite types pass (High on the pairing, Medium on type 179 meaning difficulty: its ObjectIds run 1 to 24 like Creature2Difficulty).

## Special cases that stand in for an unknown generic mechanism

- **Settler minfrastructure.** Only the broken or unbuilt objects are in the world data (Broken Banner Exile and so on); retail swapped in the repaired object with a lost server script. 90 broken to repaired pairs, matched by Creature2 description and, where no name matches, by 3D model (the same mesh grown or built, a _grow mesh to its grown mesh, a dirt mound to plants) (WorldEntity.RepairedMinfrastructure); using the broken one puts the repaired one in its place for 15 minutes (invented), then the broken one returns.
- **Discoveries.** Discovery - Tier 1 to 4 - Indicator markers only cast Uncovering Discovery (path XP for Explorers, a visual, then despawn); what retail revealed under them was server side and is lost, and no Discovery Stash or chest has a recorded drop table. Uncovering one gives one roll from the salvage loot at the player's level and the marker stays away 30 minutes (both invented, WorldEntity.DiscoveryIndicators).
- **Sibling loot.** A creature with no drop table uses the table of a creature with the same Creature2 description and faction (LootGroupManager.BorrowSiblingLoot), for example the Northern Wilds Dominion Scout uses the Dominion Gunner's. Failing that, a creature of the same UnitRaceId within 10 levels, preferring the same archetype (invented). Critters are not given loot. As of 2026-10-21 about 68 percent of placed non-critter enemies drop something; the rest are mostly level 30 to 50 zones with few archived pages.

These fix a visible symptom without knowing the real rule, the kind of patch that piles up. Each should be replaced when the mechanism is understood.

- **Quest creatures despawn after activation** (WorldEntity.OnActivateSuccess). Narrowed in fbc02afc after decoding SetBusy: it now only applies when the activate spell has neither a DespawnUnit nor a timed SetBusy clear, the cases retail finished with server scripts (the Trapped Survivor spell 1817 has only Activate).
- **Hidden script steps complete themselves.** Activate steps with no target or location and either no text (341 game wide) or the Hidden flag with a designer note as text (last step of Contact with Thayd) were finished by lost scripts; they complete once every other step is done (QuestManager.UpdateAreaObjectives). Type 10 steps with no target and the Hidden flag (From the Ashes of Greenbough's fifth planting, Predators and Prey's fourth cage; 36 game wide) were scripted updates and complete the same way, at their full count.
- **Places with nothing to activate fill within 20 s.** An activate step with no target but a map indicator (Follow the power cables) gained one count per second inside the indicator. The Protectors of Celestion asks for 3500, a reputation bar retail filled by hunting animals while Aurin Hunters watch, a lost script. Each second inside the indicator now adds Count/20, so any such step fills after 20 s of holding the place (QuestManager.HoldProgress).
- **Gather objectives count killing or using the node.** GatheResource objectives (10, such as the Fallen Trees in A Fiery Escape) name a harvest node; real harvesting needs tradeskills, so destroying or activating the creature counts (UnitEntity kill credit, WorldEntity.OnActivateSuccess). A HarvestUnit has no health, so the node is used directly (WorldEntity.IsActiveQuestActivateTarget lists GatheResource).

Lowland Lavender Eggs counts the eggs, Ravenok Racer is a timed run from the start to the finish line (the racing birds are not simulated).
- **Auroria scans and challenges.** BIOLOGY: Victims of Augmentation scans Collegium Corpses at the Windfall dig, the augmented researchers named in its text do not exist. Terminite Studies takes the Larval Terminites and eggs, Heroes of the Osun one scan of each of the five Osun shrines. Speeder Beater, Hot Hand and Here Comes the Boom count their creatures (dropping the explosive is the activation), Skeech Saboteur is a timed run to the totem.
- **Illium.** ANALYSIS: Eldan Treasure takes one scan of each of the eight Luminous Gardens holostatues. Capital Trash counts the market litter. Chancellor Juro (the Drusera quests and Alpha Sanctum Expedition), Axis Pheydra (also in OMNICore-1 for Journey into OMNICore-1), Emperor Myrcalus and the Enigma Chamber Caretaker of the world story quests have no spawns and stand at their quests' location links (data/missions.sql), so they may be outside the rooms they belong in.
- **Timed runs.** A General challenge whose start spot differs from its indicator and whose tiers are all time limits (Whirlwind Racer, Ravenok Racer, Skeech Saboteur) is a run from the start spot to the indicator, whatever the text asks on the way (ChallengeManager.IsTimedRun). Can You Dig It? completes at the treasure location. Flitterfly Chaser counts the Everstar Flitterflies passed within 4 m. Eldan Enigma (Levian Bay) takes 6 scans of any of the Security Hub Sigma Eldan devices.
- **Creature objectives may name a target group.** About 80 kill, talk, activate and interaction objectives carry a TargetGroup id in Data instead of a Creature2 id. When Data is no creature, any creature of that target group counts (Quest.NamesTargetGroupOf), and objectives whose Data is their own reward pane group are counted once through the reward pane.
- **Checklist objectives are bitmasks.** Objective types 10 and 14 (Overload the Dominion Cannons in Shellshock!, Burn Skeech Huts in Fiery Distraction) names the target group of its units. Each used unit sets the bit of its QuestChecklistIdx, and the objective completes once Count bits are set, the way the client reads type 14 progress (WildStar64.exe 0x1405517a0). Checklist units stay in the world after use so the client can show them used (QuestObjective.IsChecklist, WorldEntity.OnActivateSuccess).
- **Burned huts show a fire unit.** Fiery Distraction's nine fire units (16726, the retail Invisible Fire Scaling Unit) stand at the huts with matching checklist indices and show once that hut's bit is set (prerequisite type 94 on the unit). The Growing Flames scale spell retail cast on them is not used, so the fire keeps its base size.
- **Generated spawns carry checklist indices.** Spawns for checklist objective units get a QuestChecklistIdx: units of a target group with several creatures take their position in it (the Contact with Thayd signal flares 1 to 3), copies of a lone creature take their copy number. The three signal flares stand only at their own objective markers (build_spawns.py CHK, DROPAT).
- **Communicator offers by prerequisite.** Communicator messages with no quest state condition that offer a quest with prerequisite quests (Contact with Thayd after Fiery Distraction) are checked when a quest is completed and on entering the world, and the quest is mentioned once its prerequisites and the message's own conditions are met. Messages without a world or whose quest has no prerequisite quests are left alone, many of those are beta or unused (GlobalQuestManager.InitialiseCommunicatorPrerequisites).
- **In-game store quests complete on accept.** Learning to Shop (10510) asks for an OmniBit purchase in the retail store, which is gone, so its objectives complete when it is accepted (QuestManager.LostServiceQuests).
- **Generated spawn heights checked against the ground.** Spawns placed from Jabbithole pins took their height from the nearest retail spawn, which buried some on slopes (the Contact with Thayd signal flares were 3 to 4.5 m underground). Every generated spawn was compared with the ground height from tools/heights and with its nearest retail spawn: 208 that were 1 to 8 m buried or floating while a surface retail spawn stood within 25 m were moved to the ground. Larger gaps (cliffs, overhangs, caves) were left alone (wildstar-archive tools/height_fixes.tsv, read by build_spawns.py).
- **Moved story NPCs.** Retail moved some story NPCs between camps with server phasing that is lost. Where only the later copy has a visibility prerequisite, the earlier copy is hidden once the quest that sends the player after them is taken: the hub Deadeye Brightland (11063) after Calm Before the Storm (Player.MovedStoryNpcs).
- **Panels that opened doors.** A lost server script opened some doors from control panels. Panels listed in WorldEntity.ControlledDoors open or close their door within range when used: the Barrier Control Panel at Camp Icefury opens the Icefury Fence Door in front of the Dominion ships.
- **Path switch refreshes nearby units.** After a path switch the server re-sends every visible non-player unit (Player.ResendVisibleEntities from PathManager.ActivatePath). This was added for settler resources, but the client re-evaluates path prerequisites on quest updates anyway (2026-10-20 binary reading); the real settler resource bug was our spawns using entity type 0 instead of CollectableUnit (8). Kept as harmless.
- **Crowd control ends on death and never outlives its duration.** Retail cleared stuns, roots and the like on death; we do the same (UnitEntity.OnDeath). On top of that the stunned unit expires a state itself a second after its effect's duration, or as soon as the applying spell has finished, because an effect whose caster died or despawned is never ticked to its end by our spell system (CrowdControlManager.Update). A player can therefore be stunned at most one second longer than the effect says, never shorter.
- **Quest reputation.** A completed quest grants reputation with Quest2.Faction2IdRewardReputation00/01: RewardReputationOverride when set, else 100 + 10 x con level (invented; retail amounts per quest are not in our data). Quests whose Quest2Reward rows already carry reputation use those rows instead.
- **Enter-area objectives without an indicator are zone checks.** EnterArea objectives with no WorldLocation2 indicator treat their Data as a WorldZone id: standing in it (or a sub zone) completes them.
- **Mentioned and abandoned quests read as not started by prerequisites.** Quest state prerequisites (NPC visibility, quest availability) treat a quest the communicator merely offered (Mentioned) or one the player abandoned as state 0. Retail's phasing presumably did the same for Kormak, whose visibility rule only names states 0 and 1.
- **Cinematic objectives.** An ActivateEntity objective with Data 0, no reward pane target group and no map indicator completes on accept (QuestManager.QuestAdd). Retail played a client cinematic there (They're Back!: "Look to the sky").
- **Quest receivers stay visible.** A creature hidden by its visibility prerequisite is shown anyway while the player carries an accepted or achieved quest in its Creature2.QuestIdReceive list (Player.ReceivesActiveQuest). Retail phased story NPCs per player; two Kormaks may stand together for a moment here.
- **Path missions start at their indicators.** An unlocked path mission moves to Started when the player stands within 60 m (or the indicator's own radius) of one of its PathMission.WorldLocation2Id00-03 indicators (PathMissionManager.StartMissionsAtIndicators), besides its first progress. Prerequisite type 107 with a PathMission id as ObjectId compares the mission state like type 155. Retail started missions on entering their area with server logic we do not have.
- **Quest abilities.** The spell a SpellSuccess objective names (QuestObjective.Data) is castable through ClientCastObjectiveAbility while an accepted quest has that objective open; it needs no ability book entry and ends with the objective (ClientCastObjectiveAbilityHandler). Retail's grant and removal of these abilities is unknown; the tracker button is the only client surface we know.
- **Direct activation of quest targets.** A unit with no activation spell is used directly (WorldEntity.OnActivateCast); when an accepted quest of the user targets it with an activation, interaction (SucceedCSI) or checklist objective, that use is the activation success and credits the quest. Retail reached these units through quest-granted spells (Fiery Vengeance's incendiary grenades on the slaver shacks) that our data does not grant.
- **Knockbacks keep their height under cover.** A forced move (knockback, pull, leap) lands at the terrain heightmap only when that is at most 4 m above the mover; a bigger gap means the mover stands under an overhang, in a cave or inside a building, and the computed height is kept (SpellEffectForcedMoveHandler.TerrainSnapLimit). Retail used collision geometry we do not have; the heightmap alone put Inventor Hoom on the surface 128 m above his lab.
- **Area marker groups without markers use the objective's indicator.** When none of a type 9 target group's marker entities is spawned, a kill within 100 m of the kill objective's map indicator (QuestObjective.WorldLocationsIdIndicator) counts for the group. Retail's markers for Yeti Extermination (the Icemaw Cavern cave entrances) are not in our data; the indicator sits at the cavern.
- **Marker groups name talk targets too.** A type 9 target group behind a talk step names the creatures to talk to (A Fiery Escape's Trapped Aurin, Frazzle and Frizz, one spawn each). Talking to them credits the group like a creature group (AssetManager.CacheCreatureTargetGroups), and the bot talks to them whenever one is spawned instead of looking for hostiles near a marker.
- **Standing in a zone.** For zone entry calls a player is in a zone when the map file's area says so, or when a WorldLocation2 row of that zone (or a sub zone) lies within 60 m; the map areas and the WorldLocation2 rows disagree in places (Tremor Ridge's ridge, zone 1083, is area 23 in the map file). The check also runs after every local teleport.
- **Zone entry communicator calls.** A communicator call whose only quest condition is the offered quest itself (state 0, never started) and that names a world zone is treated as "call when the player enters that zone"; the zone check walks the player's zone and its parents. Retail's exact trigger for these rows is unknown (the WorldZoneId check was commented out upstream as "trigger when the player gets here"); this reading matches A Hidden Threat.
- **Zone prerequisites use the zone check.** An InSubZone prerequisite (type 26) is met by the same rule as "standing in a zone" below: the map area, or a WorldLocation2 row of the zone family within 60 m.
- **SWAT gadgets.** Retail soldiers used a gadget (a virtual item) on SWAT mission targets. Here a kill of the target counts, and so does using it, since some targets are objects that cannot be attacked (Fire in the Hole's canimid burrows).
- **Faction and race target groups.** TargetGroup types 3 (factions) and 12 (creature races) resolve to every Creature2 entry of those factions or races; objectives and missions built on them count any such creature.
- **Group content stays group content.** WANTED [GROUP 2+] bosses (Creature2Difficulty 6) and the Settler Sheriff missions built on them are not scaled down for a solo player (Brian, 2026-11-03); the quest bot reports them SKIPPED.
- **Public event quests are out of the bot's reach.** Quests whose prerequisite needs a running public event (type 117) are reported SKIPPED by the quest bot; the events themselves are tracked separately.
- **Units re-sent after entering the world.** Once loading ends the server re-sends every visible non-player unit (Player.OnEnteredWorld). Added for the same wrong settler resource theory as the path switch refresh; kept as harmless.

## EnterArea objectives without a marker

Retail completed "enter area" objectives with map trigger volumes the server does not have. An objective with a map marker completes inside the marker (radius at least 10 m). One without a marker completes within 25 m of a creature its text names, within 30 m of a location its text links, or anywhere in the world zone its Data names (including sub zones). Approximation: the retail volume may have been smaller or larger than these.

## The quest bot wears gear of its level

A player of level N wears quest and vendor gear of about power level N. The bot has no quest-reward or vendor flow, so after each level raise it adds (GM `!item add`) and equips a plain green greatsword, heavy armor set and shield of its level: the highest power level at or below the level among Item2 rows of quality 2 with no class, quest or flag lock (shields carry flag 0x4000 on every plain one, allowed). Each replaced piece is deleted as a player vendors it. Approximation: real gear is green to blue with stats; the bot's has the slot's base armor, health and shield only, so it is a floor for a player's survivability, not a ceiling.

- **Open-ended crowd control (2026-11-09).** A durationless crowd control from a spell that never finishes would last forever. An AbilityRestriction of that kind outside a vehicle or transformation is released after 120 s, and one from a quest spell ends when the quest leaves the accepted state. The Vegging Out Transmorphing form (34861) ends when its vegetable-gathering step (objective 2) completes, since the original script that ended it is lost.
- **EnterZone with no indicator (2026-11-09).** The step completes in the named zone or in any area whose parent chain contains it.
