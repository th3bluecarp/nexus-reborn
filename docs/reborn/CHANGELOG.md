# Changelog (Nexus Reborn)

Newest first. "Live" means running on our test server.

## 2026-10-21

- **Settler minfrastructure repairs** (923991f8): broken or unbuilt Settler objects (banners, fences, firepits, flower pots, lamps, pylons, mile markers and more, 57 kinds) now turn into their repaired version when you fix them, and stay repaired for 15 minutes before the broken one returns. Before, the broken banner stayed broken and could be clicked forever.
- **Creatures that dropped nothing** (0846247a): creatures with no recorded drop table, such as the Northern Wilds Dominion Scout, now drop what their sibling creatures of the same kind drop (the scout uses the Dominion Gunner's table).
- **Discoveries** (e7c18e9c): uncovering a Discovery marker now gives a small find (one roll from the salvage table at your level: materials, rune fragments) and the marker stays gone for 30 minutes. Before it gave nothing and came back a minute later.
- **AMPs and talents that change other abilities** (ee8d64ff): cooldown cuts (Path Expert, Swift Gadgetry, Impulse), cost cuts (Efficiency, abilities that are free for a while), cast time changes and damage or healing boosts to specific abilities or groups (fire and water spells, Atomic Rush, Building Pressure) now apply. Decoded from the game's modifier tables. Duration changes are not applied yet.
- **Combat stats use the game client's own math** (1f680521): every combat rating (critical hit chance and severity, deflect, strikethrough, glance, multi-hit, lifesteal, reflect, armor pierce) counted 100 times too strongly, and they had no soft cap. They now convert exactly as the client's character sheet does. Fights at low level change most: crits land about as often as your sheet says and hit for about 150 percent, not 250.
- **Damage formula** (083a8218): the game's 150 percent base crit damage was missing (a crit with no Critical Hit Severity on gear did no damage at all). Fixed, and the combat stats that did nothing now work: Strikethrough lowers the target's Deflect chance, Armor Pierce ignores part of the target's armor, Critical Hit Deflect and Critical Mitigation protect the target, Multi-Hit adds extra damage, Lifesteal heals you, Reflect sends damage back, and glancing blows show how much was glanced. Also from earlier today: damage and healing marks (85298f28).
- **Cheat-death and on-death AMPs** (dc650c17): Last Stand, To the Pain, Unbreakable, Homeward Bound and The Trick now save you from a killing blow and cast their effect; Final Salvo, From the Grave, Cheat Death and the PvP Self-Destruct fire as you die. Their "once every N minutes" lockouts are respected. Not yet tried in game.
- **Immunities** (4e00a058): Freedom, Unstoppable Force, Self CC Immunity and boss immunities now block the crowd control, knockbacks, interrupts or damage they name.
- **Damage and healing marks** (85298f28): abilities that make a foe take more damage from you (Analyze Weakness, Cheap Shot, Nano Spike), deal less damage to you (Nerve Induction) or boost healing you give someone (Sigil of Recovery, Fusion Probe) now change the numbers. About 130 class and AMP spell effects use this.
- **Cooldown reduction and reset effects** (fd3d0f9b): AMPs and abilities that shorten, reset or set another ability's cooldown now do it.
- **Fixes from a review of today's work** (e4e1245a): the commodity exchange only trades items with a normal credit price (it could sell Renown or Prestige items for a few copper), at most 999 per order; salvage only works on items in your bags (salvaging worn gear kept its stats); a trade is cancelled if an offered stack was split after offering; rune slot inputs, vehicle boarding range (15 m) and a rename race are checked.
- **Explorer Searching ability** (bdb6de00) now answers with the hot or cold search effect instead of being ignored.
- **Settler projects change the world** (c5b98030): things that should only appear once a Settler infrastructure project is built (or only before it) now follow your project's state.
- **Runes checked in game** (554a6af7): the Runecrafting window first showed no slots; the game hides an item's rune slots unless a version marker is set in its random-stat data, which is now sent. Tested with a throwaway character: slots show, putting a rune in works ("Inscribing operation successful", the rune leaves your bags) and adding a slot of a chosen element works.
- **Four more prerequisite checks** (8a836a5c): riding a vehicle, being alive or dead, carrying a number of an item, and path level now gate NPCs, quests and spells the way the data says (they were always treated as met).
- **Salvage tables rebuilt from 190 archived items** (f820467e, was 61), so the odds per level band are steadier.
- **Fewer ignored messages** (05ff6ad2): answering or dismissing a communicator call and the CREDD exchange check at login are now handled (both showed as unhandled every session).
- **Runes** (6774d20c): armor and weapons (item level 5 and up) now have rune slots, using the game's own rules for how many and which elements (your current gear gets its slots the next time you log in). The Runecrafting window works: put runes in matching slots (Fusion runes fit anywhere), take them out (optionally keeping the rune), add slots and change empty slots' elements, all free for now. Rune stats count while the gear is equipped.
- **Placed vehicles can be boarded** (08456a7b): clicking a vehicle that stands in the world (Farside battle suits, the Rage Logic shiphand speeders) puts you in the pilot seat; getting out leaves it where it was for the next use.
- **Wake Here has its 30 minute cooldown** (dce92dff). It was free and unlimited; it is still free, but after using it the holocrypt (or a service token) is the way back for 30 minutes, as in late retail. The death window shows the time left.
- **XP boost buffs and consumables work** (62a77f66): Combat XP increased and similar boosts add their bonus to the XP you earn, and other boost types show on your character.
- **Disguises keep creatures calm** (f8118b0a): disguise spells that change your standing with a faction (Hidden in Plain Sight, Hidden in Hay) now stop that faction's creatures from attacking you on sight.
- **Commodity Exchange** (603a7621): run by the server as an unlimited market. Buy Now and Sell Now work for any stackable item at server prices (vendor price to buy, a fifth of it to sell, never less than a vendor pays).
- **Salvaging gear gives materials** (88cbe017): salvage tables for every armor, weapon and gadget type and level, rebuilt from what players recorded on Jabbithole (for example light armor at levels 5 to 10: Canvas Cloth 41 percent, Rough Quartz Power Core 15, Nano Tech Parts 13, rune signs and fragments).
- **Vendor discount buffs** (3aa0cb44) lower prices and raise what vendors pay.
- **Trading between players** (457d3d1c): invite, offer up to 8 items and credits, both commit and the swap happens. Several trade message layouts were wrong and are fixed from the game client.
- **Galactic Archive** (415884ad): the journal's lore articles now unlock and are saved. Articles the game unlocks itself (talking to certain creatures, archive links) are recorded, and entries tied to finishing a quest unlock when you finish it, including quests you finished before today.
- **Explorer missions** listen to the game's own "node reached" reports (e97ac111), on top of the server's distance check.
- **Combat log filters** (c87b3396): the combat log checkboxes and Hide Other Players now stop the server sending those entries.
- **Zone, LFG, class and guild recruitment chat** (8485b11a) work instead of answering "Currently not implemented". Zone chat reaches everyone on the same continent.
- **Quests that reward tradeskill xp pay it** (b54fd665), to a tradeskill you have learned (378 quests).
- **Four more spell effects** (84f3c875): items handed out by spells, Dismount and other knock-off-your-mount effects, instant kills, and spells that advance an achievement.
- **Gear durability and vendor repairs** (66cf2d84). Dying now costs your equipped armor and weapons 10 percent durability (our number, retail's is unknown), shown on your gear at once. Repair and Repair All at vendors work and charge exactly the price the vendor window shows (formula read from the game client).
- **Salvage is wired up** (66cf2d84). The game's Salvage action is the same message that opens loot bags; armor and weapons now break down into their salvage table. The salvage tables themselves are being rebuilt from archived Jabbithole data, so gear salvage yields nothing until that data lands.
- **Rename a character** from the character select screen (c5cc0eb2). Free; a character that owns a house cannot be renamed yet.
- **Duels** (971a149b): challenge a targeted player, accept or decline, 5 second countdown, the loser drops to 1 health instead of dying. Forfeit or wandering 50 m away for 10 seconds ends it.
- **Ride and quest vehicle spells work** (e4d60c36). The SummonVehicle spell effect was ignored. It now spawns the vehicle and seats you as the pilot for the ride spells (hoverbike, warpig, battle bot, securitybot); Exit Vehicle gets you out and the vehicle goes away.
- **Turn PvP On/Off** from your portrait menu now works (e4d60c36). Players only fight each other when both are flagged. Turning it off is immediate, retail had a cooldown.
- **Reset Instances** works (e4d60c36): your next dungeon entry starts a fresh copy. The instance you are standing in is kept.
- **The ability trainer's Learn button** teaches a class ability you qualify for but do not know yet, for free (e4d60c36). Abilities still come automatically on level up.
- **Tracker corrected**: harvest nodes, Soldier holdouts, Explorer missions, path XP and work orders were already built but still listed as missing.

- **Northern Wilds could stop letting you log in** (live). When the game unloaded an empty part of the map, one creature that had already left the map was still listed there, and the unload crashed on it every update. That part of the map got stuck, and logging in there timed out, so the client kept losing connection. The unload now drops such leftovers and carries on.

## 2026-10-20

- **Settler improvements are permanent** (live, our choice). Built improvements no longer expire after 5 minutes. Retail timing is one switch away (PermanentImprovements), noted in SEMANTICS.
- **Settler depot window shows its button and timer** (live). The server now sends each improvement's build status, so the build button reads Build (it was blank) and built improvements show their time left. Building one that is already up adds another 5 minutes for the top-up cost, up to 10 times.
- **Settler resource nodes disappear when gathered** and come back after the respawn delay (e1502ab4, live).

- **Settler depots open the build window** (live). Using a depot sent a request the server ignored, so you could not spend Salvaged Machine Parts on improvements. The server now answers with the build window message (0x66D, layout read from the client).

- **Settler resources and other collectables can be used** (data only, live). Our spawn data created all 310 collectable objects (Creature2 CreationTypeEnum 8, such as Salvaged Machine Parts) as NonPlayer entities, so the client never offered to use them. They are now CollectableUnit entities, the same as the 4,383 in the world database. The spawn builders write the type from the creature data.
